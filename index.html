    Yeh kaise nikalta hai?

    Gaane ke average spectrum ko ek aam, balanced master se compare kiya jata hai. 3–8 kHz ka zyada hona, 16 kHz par awaaz kat jana, dabi hui dynamics aur dhundhli beech ki awaaz AI gaano mein aam milte hain. Yeh sirf awaaz ke patterns se andaza hai, pakka saboot nahi. Bass-heavy genre mein bass wali line ko ignore kar sakte ho.

    Studio Master

    Ek click mein tone, tez awaaz, bass, upar ki hawa, stereo aur loudness ek mastering studio jaisi theek hoti hai. Uske baad bass aur EQ apni marzi se badal sakte ho.

    Yeh kya karta hai aur kya nahi?
    • Tone theek: gaane ka sur-balance ek achhe master jaisa karta hai, aur neeche ki gunj hataata hai.
    • Kaanch hatao: 3.6 kHz ke upar ke hisse ko do bhaag mein baant ke tab dabata hai jab awaaz chubhti hai, aur beech beech mein dikhne wali chubhti hui narrow awaazon (metallic ringing) ko kaat deta hai. Zor slider se tay hota hai, 100% par sabse zyada.
    • Bass tight: bass jhoolta ho to use kasta hai.
    • Upar ki hawa: kati hui awaaz ke upar halka air jodta hai.
    • Garmi: halki analog jaisi saturation, jisse awaaz bhari lagti hai.
    • Stereo: chaudai badhata hai aur bass beech mein rakhta hai.
    • Studio room: bahut halka room reverb, jisse sab ek hi kamre ki awaaz lagti hai.

    Yeh asli studio recording nahi banata. Robotic vocal, galat notes aur kharab mix ye theek nahi kar sakta. Kati hui upar ki awaaz bhi poori wapas nahi aati. Master hone mein kuch second se ek-do minute lagte hain, gaane ki lambai par depend karta hai.
